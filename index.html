How to play
Controls

Rotate with ←/→ or A/D, mouse, touch drag, gamepad left stick / D-pad, or a connected wheel. Connect a wheel from the top bar on WebHID browsers.

Orbs
amber orb → amber pole blue orb → blue pole unstable — big boost if caught calmly; detonates while poles are red or on the core power — let it reach the core; pole contact detonates

Unstable power orbs have a lime/white ring: they heal more and detonate harder.

Overload & waves

Caught orbs add mass. Spinning fast sheds them, but sustained speed builds a red overload: the core drains and pole contact detonates. Waves change the orb mix and announce themselves with a banner; surviving a wave awards bonus score.

Progress

Your highest wave is saved. Start / Retry resumes there; New run begins from wave 1.

Settings

Use Settings to adjust touch sensitivity, audio and — when supported — wheel FFB and rotation range. Opening Help or Settings pauses play.

Magnetar

The core is collapsing. You are the last magnet in its orbit — turn to catch energy orbs, let green power fall through, and keep the event horizon from swallowing what's left.
